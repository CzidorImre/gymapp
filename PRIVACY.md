# GymApp privacy policy

_Last updated: 4 October 2026_

GymApp (Android app `com.czidorimre.gymapp`) is a workout and body-weight tracker. It is built
so that your data stays on your phone.

## What GymApp collects

Nothing. GymApp has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer never
receives your workouts, body weight, measurements, notes or any other data.

## Where your data is stored

- Everything you enter is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage.
- Once a week the app saves a backup file to the **Documents/GymApp** folder on your phone.
  You can delete these files at any time.
- If you use Android's own backup to your Google account, Android may include the app's data
  in that backup. This is controlled by your phone's settings, not by GymApp.
- Exports and shared plan files only go where you send them using your phone's share menu.

## Network access

The app downloads exercise pictures and animations from the public jsDelivr content network
(cdn.jsdelivr.net). Like any download, this request reveals your IP address to jsDelivr; no
personal data is sent with it. See [jsDelivr's privacy policy](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net).

## Notifications

Workout-day reminders are scheduled on your phone. No server is involved.

## Children

GymApp is intended for people aged 18 and over.
